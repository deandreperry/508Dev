Standards and legal context

Do not treat WCAG and law as interchangeable.

WCAG provides technical criteria. Section 508 and ADA regulations apply in different jurisdictions and may incorporate a particular WCAG version or use WCAG as a benchmark.

Educational use: 508 Dev provides technical accessibility education, not legal advice. Legal requirements depend on jurisdiction, facts, and current authority.

Federal ICT

Section 508

The Revised 508 Standards incorporate WCAG 2.0 Level A and AA success criteria and conformance requirements by reference for covered ICT.

State and local government

ADA Title II

The DOJ web and mobile app rule incorporates WCAG 2.1 Level A and AA for covered content, subject to its exceptions and current compliance dates.

Private public accommodations

ADA Title III

No single nationwide web-specific WCAG conformance rule operates like the Title II rule. Application can depend on jurisdiction and facts.
